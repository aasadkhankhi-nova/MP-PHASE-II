Privacy Policy & Terms of Service · Last updated: October 2026

1. What Design2List is

Design2List is a listing-management tool for Etsy print-on-demand sellers. It works with your own designs and photos and connects only to shops whose owners authorize it through Etsy's official sign-in; it never copies, scrapes or reuses other shops' listings, photos or designs. It places your designs on your mockup photos, drafts SEO text (title, tags, description, alt text) that you review and edit, and creates listings on your connected Etsy shop as hidden drafts. Nothing is ever published automatically — a listing goes live only when you explicitly press Publish. The service is provided "as is", without warranties.

2. Data we store

Your account details (name, email) and workspace data (stores, mockup photos, design files, sets, listing drafts and their SEO text) are stored in our cloud database and file storage (Supabase) so you can sign in from any device. Generated product photos and videos stay in your own browser until you publish them to Etsy. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

3. Etsy connection

If you connect an Etsy shop, you authorize Design2List through Etsy's official OAuth 2.0 flow. For your shop we act only as your service provider.

What we access: your shop name and ID, your listings (titles, descriptions, tags, prices, photos, videos, variations and inventory, personalization questions), shop sections and your shipping, processing and return profiles. Permissions requested: listings_r, listings_w, listings_d, shops_r, shops_w. We do not access orders, payments or buyer details, and we never ask for or store your Etsy password.

How we use it: only for actions you start yourself — loading your listings, saving your edits, creating drafts, deleting a listing you choose to delete, and publishing when you press Publish. We never use Etsy data for analytics, AI training or any other purpose, and never share or sell it.

How long we keep it: access tokens are stored server-side only and are never visible in the browser. Shop data is refreshed from Etsy at least every 10 minutes and is cached only as long as needed to show it to you. Disconnecting your shop (Settings → Etsy → Disconnect, or in your Etsy account under Settings → Apps) deletes the tokens immediately.

Security: if we ever find that data from your shop may have been exposed, we will tell you and Etsy within 24 hours.

Etsy's own fees and policies apply to everything on Etsy. We never charge for features that Etsy provides for free. If we ever stop offering the Etsy connection, we will give you at least 30 days' notice.

4. AI keys

If you add your own AI API key (Google, OpenAI, or another provider) for SEO text, that key is kept in your own browser's storage only — it is never saved on our servers. AI requests are sent through our server directly to your chosen provider and are not stored.

5. Third-party services

We use: Supabase (database, login, file storage), Render (application server), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Google (optional sign-in; optional AI provider), Cloudflare (bot protection), and Etsy (only if you connect your shop).
