ListPilot

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service · Last updated: September 2026

1. What ListPilot is

ListPilot is a listing-management tool for print-on-demand sellers. It places your designs on your mockup photos, drafts SEO text (title, tags, description, alt text) that you review and edit, and creates listings on your connected Etsy shop as hidden drafts. Nothing is ever published automatically — a listing goes live only when you explicitly press Publish. The service is provided "as is", without warranties.

2. Data we store

Your account details (name, email) and workspace data (stores, mockup photos, design files, sets, listing drafts and their SEO text) are stored in our cloud database and file storage (Supabase) so you can sign in from any device. Generated product photos and videos stay in your own browser until you publish them to Etsy. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

3. Etsy connection

If you connect an Etsy shop, you authorize ListPilot through Etsy's official OAuth 2.0 flow. The access tokens are stored server-side only, are never visible in the browser, and are used only for actions you start yourself (loading your listings, saving your edits, creating your drafts, publishing when you press Publish). Disconnecting your shop deletes the tokens. Etsy's own fees and policies apply to everything on Etsy.

4. AI keys

If you add your own AI API key (Google, OpenAI, or another provider) for SEO text, that key is kept in your own browser's storage only — it is never saved on our servers. AI requests are sent through our server directly to your chosen provider and are not stored.

5. Third-party services

We use: Supabase (database, login, file storage), Render (application server), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Google (optional sign-in; optional AI provider), Cloudflare (bot protection), and Etsy (only if you connect your shop).
